# Delivery Roadmap

## MVP Scope

Build one polished US immigration vertical slice before expanding content. The slice includes Coach Mode, Real-World Mode, repeat and slower-speech controls, transcript review, structured feedback, Pragati progress, targeted Uccharan drills, and Shabd phrase review.

## Delivery Phases

| Phase | Outcome | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Curriculum blueprint and voice prototype | Stable audio session, scripted exchange, transcript capture |
| Vertical slice | US immigration end to end | Both modes, evaluation, retry loop, progress update |
| MVP expansion | Four additional missions | Hotel, restaurant, directions, and baggage flows pass user testing |
| Beta | Learning and reliability evidence | Representative Indian learners complete sessions on target devices |
| Launch | Freemium consumer release | Onboarding, retention, support, privacy, and payment paths are operational |

## Team

The minimum cross-functional team covers product, mobile, realtime backend, applied AI, curriculum, and product design. Speech expertise and representative user research are essential; they should not be deferred until after scoring and correction behavior are built.

## Release Gates

Before widening access, verify:

- acceptable turn latency and interruption behavior on low-end devices and variable networks;
- fair transcript and scoring performance across Indian speech patterns;
- scenario completion without hidden operator intervention;
- understandable, non-judgmental feedback;
- consent, retention, deletion, and support workflows;
- evidence that targeted practice improves a later mission attempt.

## Primary Risks

Voice latency, unfair speech recognition, generic curriculum, harsh feedback, weak privacy controls, and novelty-driven retention are the principal risks. Mitigate them with narrow scenarios, calibrated audit sets, delayed feedback, explicit data choices, and short daily practice tied to visible readiness gains.

Dates and budget ranges in the research report are planning assumptions, not commitments. Re-estimate them after the voice prototype and moderated user tests establish actual latency, model usage, content-production effort, and support needs.
