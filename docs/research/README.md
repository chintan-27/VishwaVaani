# Research

The [deep research report](deep-research-report.md) is the evidence base for VishwaVaani’s product, learning, architecture, privacy, and go-to-market decisions. It includes competitor analysis, CEFR-aligned learning objectives, a proposed scenario schema, voice-stack comparisons, scoring criteria, privacy considerations, and an illustrative first-year plan.

Use the report when:

- validating or revising a product recommendation;
- tracing a decision to its supporting market or pedagogy research;
- reviewing detailed JSON schemas, diagrams, budgets, or risk tables;
- distinguishing sourced facts from planning assumptions.

The shorter documents elsewhere under `docs/` are decision-oriented summaries. When they conflict with the research report, resolve the discrepancy explicitly: update the summary if it drifted, or record why new implementation or user evidence supersedes the original recommendation.

## Source Handling

Preserve source attribution when moving or paraphrasing factual claims. Clearly label costs, dates, conversion expectations, and market forecasts as assumptions unless independently verified. For time-sensitive claims—such as model capabilities, laws, prices, or competitor features—recheck the original source before relying on them.
